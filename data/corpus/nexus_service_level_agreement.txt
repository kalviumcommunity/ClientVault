1. INCIDENT SEVERITY CLASSIFICATIONS
- Severity 1 (Critical Outage): Core transaction settlement system down.
  Initial response time <= 15 minutes. Update cadence every 30 minutes.
- Severity 2 (Major Degradation): Non-critical service unavailable or redundant pipeline degraded.
  Initial response time <= 1 hour.
- Severity 3 (Minor Defect / General Inquiry): Standard operational questions or administrative tasks.
  Initial response time <= 4 business hours.
