1. Circuit Breaker Trigger Thresholds

The gateway circuit breaker automatically trips to OPEN state when downstream processor failure rates exceed 4.5% over a rolling 60-second window or when settlement latency exceeds 1200ms.

2. Gateway Failover Sequence

  1. Verify secondary clearing network route health via nexus-cli gateway ping --target processor-beta.
  2. Execute traffic switch: nexus-cli route switch --from processor-alpha --to processor-beta --drain-timeout 30s.
  3. Inspect ledger reconciliation logs to confirm zero dropped transaction payloads during in-flight drain.
  4. Alert Nexus Treasury desk that settlement batches will clear under secondary acquirer profile.

Compliance Notice: SOC2 and PCI-DSS dual cryptographic authorization required for manual rerouting.
