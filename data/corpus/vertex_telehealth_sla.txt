--- Document Header: Vertex Health Telehealth SLA Confidential ---

1. SYSTEM AVAILABILITY & SERVICE METRICS
Vertex Health Systems requires 99.95% monthly operational uptime across the core Telehealth WebRTC video routing infrastructure and Electronic Health Record integration gateway.

2. INCIDENT SEVERITY AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE COMMITMENTS
- Severity 1 (Emergency Clinical Impact): Hospital EHR offline or live emergency telehealth consultations failing.
  Initial response time <= 30 minutes. Incident bridge manned continuously by Principal Clinical Systems Engineer.
- Severity 2 (Significant Clinical Impairment): Patient scheduling portal unavailable or digital lab results delayed.
  Initial response time <= 2 hours.
- Severity 3 (Minor Operational Inquiry): Non-urgent provider credentialing ticket or static report discrepancy.
  Initial response time <= 6 business hours.

3. REGULATORY BREACH NOTIFICATION WINDOW
In the event of an confirmed data exposure or unencrypted PHI transmission incident:
- Incident Commander must notify Vertex Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) within 60 minutes.
- Formal regulatory notification to HHS Office for Civil Rights must be coordinated within 24 hours.

4. ESCALATION CONTACT DIRECTORY
Level 1: 24/7 Clinical Service Desk (clinical-support@vertex-health.org)
Level 2: Hospital Reliability Operations Team (lead-oncall@vertex-health.org)
Level 3: VP of Medical Informatics & Chief Medical Officer
