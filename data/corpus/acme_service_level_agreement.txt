1. SERVICE AVAILABILITY COMMITMENT
The ClientVault support platform guarantees 99.9% uptime for the ACME Freight API gateway and dispatch integration systems during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

2. INCIDENT SEVERITY AND RESPONSE TIME MATRIX
- Severity 1 (Critical Dispatch Outage): Total inability to track shipments or submit customs manifest.
  Initial response time <= 30 minutes. Status updates every 45 minutes until resolution.
- Severity 2 (Major Impairment): Key reporting dashboards or bulk EDI transmission delayed.
  Initial response time <= 2 hours.
- Severity 3 (Minor Operational Issue): Individual user login failure or documentation clarification.
  Initial response time <= 8 business hours.

3. OUTAGE PENALTIES & SERVICE CREDITS
If monthly availability drops below 99.9%, ACME Logistics receives billing credits according to the following schedule:
- 99.0% to 99.89%: 10% monthly service credit.
- 95.0% to 98.99%: 25% monthly service credit.
- Below 95.0%: 50% monthly service credit and executive review within 5 business days.

4. ESCALATION CONTACTS
Level 1: Dispatch Desk Support (ops@acme-logistics.com)
Level 2: Principal Logistics Systems Engineer (oncall@acme-logistics.com)
Level 3: VP of Supply Chain Infrastructure
