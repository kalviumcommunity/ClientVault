1. Clinician Identity & Credential Verification

All healthcare practitioners requiring access to the Vertex Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform must possess verified National Provider Identifier (NPI) registration and state medical licensing credentials approved by the Hospital Credentialing Board.

2. Role-Based Access Assignment (RBAC)

  1. Attending Physicians receive Full Clinical Write permissions for assigned patient panels.
  2. Resident Physicians and Nurses receive Supervised Charting permissions requiring co-signature.
  3. Administrative and Billing Staff are confined strictly to anonymized demographic and insurance claims data.

3. Biometric Hardware MFA Enforcement

Clinicians must register a FIDO2 hardware biometric key or hospital smartcard badge before remote telehealth or chart access is permitted.

HIPAA Compliance Notice: Clinician session logs are audited daily against suspicious chart access patterns.
