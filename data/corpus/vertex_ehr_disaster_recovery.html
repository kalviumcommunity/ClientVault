1. Trigger Criteria

Failover to secondary replica region (us-east-2) must initiate only when the primary cluster experiences unrecoverable latency exceeding 5000ms or 3 consecutive heartbeat timeouts.

2. Execution Sequence

  1. Notify Incident Commander and open war-room bridge.
  2. Execute database promoter script: vertex-cli db failover --cluster prd-vtx-01 --force-replica.
  3. Update Route53 health check DNS pointers to secondary cluster target.
  4. Confirm integrity of encrypted patient data volumes before restoring application traffic.

Compliance Notice: HIPAA / HITECH cryptographic validation required post-failover.
