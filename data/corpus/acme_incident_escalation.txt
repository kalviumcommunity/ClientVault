1. INCIDENT TRIAGE & NOTIFICATION WORKFLOW
When monitoring alarms detect warehouse dispatch stoppage or EDI gateway failure:
- Step 1: Open bridge conference line #acme-ops-incident on Slack and dial-in bridge 1-800-555-ACME.
- Step 2: Page the primary on-call Freight Systems Engineer via PagerDuty policy `ACME-HIGH-SEV`.
- Step 3: Broadcast client status notice within 15 minutes of triage confirmation.

2. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
- Incident Commander: Directs technical investigation and authorizes rollback actions.
- Communications Lead: Publishes hourly status updates to client stakeholders.
- Scribe: Records chronological timeline of telemetry events and terminal commands.

3. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW REQUIREMENTS
Within 48 hours of Sev-1 mitigation, a root cause analysis (RCA) document must be submitted to the ACME Technical Oversight Committee containing:
1. Executive summary of business impact.
2. Comprehensive timeline of root cause inception and mitigation milestones.
3. Corrective action items assigned with Jira ticket IDs and target delivery sprints.
