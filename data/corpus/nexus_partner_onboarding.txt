1. INSTITUTIONAL INTAKE VERIFICATION
Before granting programmatic access to the Nexus clearing ledger, financial institution partners must complete identity verification:
1. Valid Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) registered with the Global LEI Foundation.
2. Verified SWIFT / BIC institutional routing profile.
3. Written sign-off from designated Compliance Officer approving AML/KYC profile.

2. ENVIRONMENT PROVISIONING PHASES
- Phase 1: Staging Sandbox Simulation. Partners execute 500 test micro-settlements across all currency pairs.
- Phase 2: Dual Cryptographic Key Exchange. Mutual TLS 1.3 certificates and mTLS client certs signed by approved CA.
- Phase 3: Controlled Production Ramp. Initial 30-day transaction volume capped at $250,000 USD daily clearing value.

3. ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROLS
Programmatic API credentials must be rotated every 90 calendar days. Failure to rotate triggers automated account throttling to 10% baseline throughput until renewed.
